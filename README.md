# Luciana Ali Pontual

Luciana Ali Pontual is a technology and marketing executive specializing in brand experiences, B2B events and immersive technologies, with more than a thousand projects for large companies across 16 countries. Today she applies artificial intelligence to transform the experience between companies, brands and people.

## Frameworks
- **[Intelligence becomes Experience](https://github.com/LuPontual/intelligence-becomes-experience/blob/main/ideias/intelligence-becomes-experience.md)**: quando a inteligência artificial deixa de ser ferramenta e passa a transformar a experiência de clientes, parceiros e equipes.
- **[The Intelligence Gap](https://github.com/LuPontual/intelligence-becomes-experience/blob/main/ideias/intelligence-gap.md)**: a distância entre adotar IA e transformar o negócio com inteligência.
- **[Experience Intelligence](https://github.com/LuPontual/intelligence-becomes-experience/blob/main/ideias/experience-intelligence.md)**: eventos e experiências que geram dados e aprendizado contínuo sobre clientes e parceiros.
- **[LULLIA Intelligence Index™](https://github.com/LuPontual/intelligence-becomes-experience/blob/main/ideias/lullia-intelligence-index.md)**: diagnóstico de maturidade em IA em cinco dimensões (IA, dados, marca, experiência e receita).
